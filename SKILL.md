## Panel-specific overlay

Use black/white/grey with 1–3 source-derived accents, a small subject and vast whitespace. Avoid uncontrolled glitch, full-scene tracing and ordinary pasted-on titles. If text-free delivery conflicts with character-built imagery, explain the conflict and resolve it before spending an image call; never silently replace the defining visual language.
